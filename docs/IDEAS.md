# CellScape — Owner's ideas (raw capture)

Captured as the owner dumps them; organised into a plan afterwards. Dates are when raised.

## 2026-10-09

1. **Graphics like the viral molecular animations**: real 3D, glossy, depth of field (first 2D build was below expectation). Reference: Scoolam membrane animation; Biology Notes Online reel "How the Organelles Work Together in plant cell" (translucent glassy organelles; nucleus → DNA → mRNA → ER → vesicles → Golgi; chloroplasts, mitochondria).
2. **Fully 3D and navigable**: right-click drag to change view; zoom continuously from **cell → organelle → membrane/molecular** level.
3. **Show how organelles interact through their function**, not just "here's a chloroplast, it does photosynthesis". Teaching "intro to cells" currently only names parts of animal/plant cells.
4. **Same problem for mitosis and meiosis**: diagrams/videos don't show how every part interacts with every other part.
5. **High interactivity → one simulator for Years 7–12**, with the information level adjustable by **year group and/or syllabus dot point**.
6. **Genetics pathway**, in teaching order:
   - DNA structure: double helix, nitrogenous bases, sugar-phosphate backbone (explorable model).
   - Protein synthesis (transcription → translation).
   - Protein folding.
   - Mutation: the different ways mutations occur and how each affects protein expression / the final protein.
7. **More genetics**: genes in humans, epigenetics, inheritance.
